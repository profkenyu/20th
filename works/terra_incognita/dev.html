Humanity’s journey spans three planets in search of a possible path for migration. Across the first planet, an autonomous rover recovers four structural resources and two raw materials—nitrogen and alcohol—to reinforce the lander; on the second, it determines whether water is present. Evidence gathered from both worlds re-emerges as geological memory on the third planet.
